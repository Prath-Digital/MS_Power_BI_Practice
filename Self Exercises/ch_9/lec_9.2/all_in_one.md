# 📘 Power BI Q&A – Self Excercises

## **Q1. What is the purpose of a Card visual in Power BI?**
**Answer:**  
A Card visual is used to display a single, important KPI or summary value (e.g., Total Sales, Profit, Count of Customers). It highlights key metrics clearly and prominently on a report page.

---

## **Q2. How does a Multi-Row Card differ from a single Card visual?**
**Answer:**  
A Multi-Row Card displays **multiple KPIs at once** in a list format, while a single Card shows only **one metric**. Multi-Row Cards are useful for showing several related values without requiring multiple separate cards.

---

## **Q3. In which scenarios would you prefer to use a Multi-Row Card instead of individual cards?**
**Answer:**  
Use a Multi-Row Card when:
- You want to show 3–10 KPIs in one compact visual.  
- You want consistent formatting and alignment for multiple numbers.  
- You want to reduce clutter from placing many individual cards.

---

## **Q4. What are the basic steps to create a chart visual in Power BI?**
**Answer:**  
1. Import your dataset into Power BI Desktop.  
2. Select a chart type from the Visualizations pane.  
3. Drag fields into appropriate areas (Axis, Values, Legend, etc.).  
4. Format the chart using the Visual Formatting pane.  
5. Adjust titles, colors, and labels for clarity.

---

## **Q5. List any three formatting options available for customizing chart visuals.**
**Answer:**  
- Changing colors (data colors, background, border).  
- Adjusting titles and fonts (size, weight, alignment).  
- Showing or hiding data labels, gridlines, or legends.

---

## **Q6. How can you improve the readability of chart visuals in a Power BI report?**
**Answer:**  
- Use clear titles and axis labels.  
- Apply contrasting colors for categories.  
- Enable data labels when values matter.  
- Simplify visuals by removing unnecessary gridlines or clutter.  
- Keep consistent formatting across visuals.

---

## **Q7. What kind of data is best suited for a line chart in Power BI?**
**Answer:**  
Line charts are best for **time-series or continuous data**, such as monthly sales, yearly trends, stock prices, or changes over time.

---

## **Q8. Explain how you can add a legend and data labels to a line chart.**
**Answer:**  
- **Legend:** Go to *Format → Legend → On*, then choose position and style.  
- **Data Labels:** Go to *Format → Data labels → On*, then adjust color, size, and display mode.

---

## **Q9. How does Power BI handle multiple lines in a line chart for comparison?**
**Answer:**  
Power BI automatically creates multiple lines when a field is placed into the **Legend** section. Each category becomes a separate line, allowing comparison across groups (e.g., sales by region over time).

---

## **Q10. What is the purpose of adding a trend line to a chart in Power BI?**
**Answer:**  
A trend line helps identify the overall direction of the data (upward, downward, stable). It is useful for understanding long-term patterns and making business decisions.

---

## **Q11. How do forecast lines enhance a Power BI report?**
**Answer:**  
Forecast lines help predict future values using historical data. They make reports more actionable by providing insight into expected trends, growth, or declines.

---

## **Q12. What factors influence the accuracy of forecasts in Power BI?**
**Answer:**  
- Quality and quantity of historical data.  
- Consistency and stability of past trends.  
- Seasonality patterns in the dataset.  
- Sudden external factors (market shifts, anomalies).  
- Correct configuration of forecasting parameters (confidence interval, seasonality, length).
